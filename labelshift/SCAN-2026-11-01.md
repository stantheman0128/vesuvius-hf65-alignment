# Eval offset scan (2026-11-01): what happens to the held-out score when the held-out labels move

Written on 2026-11-01 after 01:20 +0800, before any scan output existed. The save time and sha256 of this file
and of the scripts it names are in `logs/scan_timestamp.txt`. This is a descriptive add-on. It is outside
PREREG.md's rules and Amendments F to I, and it changes no run, reading or verdict of theirs.

## Why

Every training arm carries C0 labels and the same validation mask inside the held-out boxes, so every run is
scored against C0, and scoring a checkpoint with another arm's config scores it against C0 again. Two questions
need held-out labels that move:

1. Sensitivity: how far does the held-out AUC of a model trained on C0 fall when the held-out labels are moved
   by 16, 32, 64 or 128 px?
2. Does a model trained on labels moved by k px predict ink moved by k px? If it does, it scores higher on
   held-out labels moved the same way than on C0's, while the C0 model scores lower there.

## Eval label folders

`scripts/build_eval_labels.py` builds them from the same raw files, `regions.json` and villa tree as
`build_labels.py`. Each folder is the C0 arm with the held-out annotation (C0 ink labels plus the validation
mask) placed elsewhere:

- E0: unchanged.
- E4y, E8y, E16y, E32y: moved down by 4, 8, 16, 32 cells (16, 32, 64, 128 level-0 px), the move CkY makes in
  the training area.
- EAS: the held-out annotation as shipped (cell (y, x) carries canvas pixel (y, x)), the way AS reads its
  training labels.

Training supervision is C0's everywhere. The largest move (128 px) stays inside the 512 px guard, where no arm
has training supervision.

Checks before any GPU time: E0's three zarrs must be byte-identical to C0's (sha256 in
`labels/manifest_full.json`). For each moved folder, the level-0 validation mask and ink labels must equal E0's
moved by the stated amount, and E0's patch count from villa's InkDataset on the pod must be 676 for both seeds.
`scan/PRECHECK_OK` is written only after these pass, and `scan.sh` does not run any pair without it.

## Check on the GPU

Pair 1 must give C0 s27's `eval.json` back: the same patch counts, and pooled and per-segment AUC within 0.001
(`scripts/scan_check.py`). If not, the scan stops there and none of its numbers is reported as a result.

## When and what it costs

`go9b.sh` replaces `go9.sh` while it waits for ALL_DONE_H. It runs `scan.sh` after ALL_DONE_H and before
Amendment I's prepare, with the GPU to itself, then runs go9.sh's remaining steps unchanged. A pair starts only
within 35 minutes of the scan's start, and each pair has a 15 minute timeout, so Amendment I starts at most
about 50 minutes late (expected about 35). Amendment I's runs, rules and its 12:00 +0800 deadline are unchanged.
The scan does not stop Amendment I if it fails. Cost: the pod's extra time, about NT$18 to 25.

- Wording: numbers with their signs, without a significance claim. "Models trained on moved labels predict moved
  ink" may be written only if every trained checkpoint that ran gains on its own move while C0 of the same seed
  loses there. Otherwise each checkpoint is described on its own, and a one-seed result is called that.

## Limits

- Moving the held-out annotation moves the scored area with it, as a registration error does, so E0 and Ek
  score partly different patches.
- EAS differs from E0 by the AS offset (on w00 -1.6 to +17.4 px in y and -10.0 to +14.5 px in x), less than the
  32 px of E8y.
- One evaluation per pair. GPU nondeterminism is covered only by pair 1's check.
